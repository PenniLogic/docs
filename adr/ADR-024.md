## ADR-024 — Credit bureau integration declined for the current release
**Status:** ACCEPTED · 2026-10-04

**Decision.** Credit-bureau integration and score data are an **explicit non-goal
for the current release**. PenniLogic neither obtains nor presents a bureau
score. No provider, contract, purchase, endpoint, score field, import, fetch,
storage, cache, sharing category or score-movement narrative is authorized.

Debt-first progress does not promise a bureau score improvement.

**Decision authority.** The coordinating Sprint completion session
`5a1e7d78-6f81-4abb-9ab5-d8b1648df012` selected this conservative declined option
under the owner's general direction to resolve ambiguities and deliver the
original board autonomously. It is **not a separate explicit bespoke owner bureau
attestation**, provider/legal approval, or permission to spend. This implements
the declined option of [#52](https://github.com/PenniLogic/docs/issues/52),
`T-ADR-BUREAU-11`, not a replacement ticket or framework.

**Acceptance boundary.** The recorded ACCEPTED header and its registry entry are
a proposal on the author branch; this record becomes accepted source only after
separate current-head review and protected merge. It is a source decision, not
evidence of app/API enforcement, licensed legal advice, provider traffic, product
acceptance or independent review.

**Allocation.** Accepted source reserved only ADR-015 through ADR-023. The
coordinator explicitly authorized the new ADR-024 allocation for this original
ticket under [LAYOUT.md](LAYOUT.md)'s extension procedure and the existing
[Docs70](https://github.com/PenniLogic/docs/issues/70) generator's layout-owner
bootstrap. No previously reserved bureau number is claimed. The original nine
owners and allocations, existing decisions and recorded acceptance entries are
unchanged. The original `Future` sprint assignment remains unchanged.

### 1. Why decline

The debt-first wedge already has intelligible progress: the user's recorded
debt balance, deterministic payoff scenarios and interest/time comparisons under
[ADR-015](ADR-015.md) and [ADR-017](ADR-017.md). Those quantities do not establish
a bureau score, an improvement, causality or a date when a score will change.
The original ticket's motivation that paying down a card visibly moves a score
is not adopted as a product promise; reporting delays, the provider's model and
other information outside PenniLogic's ledger can affect a score.

No approved provider agreement, eligible access route, provider-specific consent
and controller/processor terms, current quote or measured incremental economics
are established by this decision. Competitive prevalence is not that evidence.
Adding regulated personal financial data and an external cost to early delivery
without those inputs is unnecessary risk. Declining is an affirmative scope
decision, not silence, a temporary outage or a queued integration.

The subscription/no-lending commitment in
[product specification section 4A.1](../product/01-product-spec.md#4a1-never-offer-credit-products-never-sell-to-a-lender)
is preserved. No lending, loan brokering or credit referral can subsidize a
future bureau feature. Historical regulatory and pricing research in
[compliance section 3.3](../compliance/01-regulatory-landscape.md#33-credit-bureau-data-cibilexperianequifaxcrif)
is diligence input, not an approved supplier, legal clearance or quote.

### 3. Options considered

| Option | Disposition and reason |
|---|---|
| Explicitly decline for the current release | **Chosen.** Keeps debt-first delivery useful without new regulated data, supplier obligations, spend or score/advice promises; the reopening gates below prevent repeated opinion-only reargument. |
| Integrate a bureau now, with stored or periodically cached scores | **Rejected.** No approved provider/legal/consent/role terms or demonstrated incremental economics; persistent copies also require an approved protection, retention, erasure and sharing design. |
| Fetch on demand, display-only, with no database retention | **Rejected.** Fetching and displaying still collect/process data and incur provider, consent, role, security and cost obligations; lack of persistence does not remove them. |
| Accept a user-entered score or imported report instead of an API | **Rejected.** Still creates a personal-financial-data collection/schema/storage/advice surface; it is not a bypass for the declined integration. |
| Infer a score or score improvement from payoff/utilization progress | **Rejected.** Ledger arithmetic is not a licensed bureau model or evidence of a causal score change; it would manufacture an unsupported progress/advice narrative. |
| Leave the question silent or indefinitely queue a default-off feature | **Rejected.** Silence reopens the same argument; a queued endpoint/schema/provider path creates obligations before there is an approved decision. No such feature is implemented here. |

### 4. Security, privacy and erasure

Bureau scores and reports would be personal financial data. While declined,
their **retention period is `NOT_APPLICABLE`**, represented by a null period,
because collection, import, fetch, storage and caching are prohibited. This is
not a zero-day retention policy that permits a transient pull.

Their **crypto-shredding classification/path is `NOT_APPLICABLE`** while absent:
no bureau column, key class or copy is introduced into the
[ADR-018](ADR-018.md) inventory or [ADR-021](ADR-021.md) erasure path.
The **bureau controller-versus-processor contract is `NOT_APPLICABLE`** because
there is no selected provider or processing arrangement. No role is guessed and
no consent/license agreement is asserted to exist.

If reopened, a reviewed minimal field and copy inventory, ADR-018 classification
and access controls, sourced retention periods, ADR-021-compatible erasure and
backup-restore behavior, and provider-specific controller/processor obligations
are mandatory before admitting data. First-party crypto-shredding must not be
misrepresented as erasing an independent controller's records. All copies,
including logs, analytics and client/server caches, must be accounted for.

These N/A statements apply only to absent bureau data. They waive none of the
existing money, append-only ledger, raw-message-on-device, default-deny sharing,
server-authoritative entitlement, optional-permission or erasure constraints.
They answer the non-goal option of threat-model finding `E26-F06`; this unit does
not edit the threat-model record or claim its workflow disposition is closed.

### 5. Sharing and the advice boundary

There is no family score access at aggregate, detail, historical, export or
offline level. No synthetic/masked score or movement can be shared instead.
Grant creation and family membership cannot override a category that is absent.
Existing lawful sharing under ADR-005/018/021 is otherwise unchanged.

The allowed score narrative set is empty for UI, notifications, exports,
marketing and AI. Debt-balance/payoff arithmetic remains bounded by
[product section 2.3](../product/01-product-spec.md#23-suggest-to-find-a-job-which-has-x-salary-vs-financial-advice-regulation)
and [compliance section 3](../compliance/01-regulatory-landscape.md#3-india--financial-advice-boundary):
scenarios and trade-offs on the user's inputs, not claims that a repayment will
raise a score or improve loan eligibility. A disclaimer cannot authorize a
prohibited score-improvement narrative or credit referral.

Author source review compares the empty narrative set and negative consequence
fixtures against those existing boundaries. This is engineering review, not
licensed legal advice or separate non-author approval; the PR must record the
actual independent review evidence before integration.

### 6. Testable reopening and future activation

`bureau-reopening-v1` in the consequences artifact publishes the exact required
evidence identifiers and acceptance conditions. **Every gate is required.**
Competitor requests, elapsed time, a provider advertisement, consent alone or a
single favorable cost estimate do not reopen or enable the feature.

| Evidence identifier | What a future reviewer must be able to test |
|---|---|
| `REOPEN-NEED` | Reproduce a comparison against debt-balance/payoff progress from a recorded protocol, sample and metrics. Numerical go/no-go thresholds are set before measurement and the results meet them; no score-improvement promise is part of the value claim. |
| `REOPEN-PROVIDER-LEGAL` | Inspect current qualified licensed legal review of the named provider's eligible access route and signed display/use/licensing terms, including downstream limits. No mandatory lending, brokering, referral or marketing arrangement is accepted. |
| `REOPEN-CONSENT-ROLE` | Inspect approved provider-specific purpose, consent/withdrawal and transfer terms; identify controller versus processor responsibilities, rights handling and each party's retention/erasure obligations explicitly. |
| `REOPEN-ECONOMICS` | Reproduce a dated quoted cost model using integer minor units plus currency, cadence, retries, failures, minimum commitments and all incremental costs against the current [published margin model](../architecture/03-stack-and-monetization.md#1-unit-economics--pricing). The new decision names numeric margin go/no-go thresholds, bounded usage and spend caps, and demonstrates they are met without credit-referral revenue. No historical research price is substituted for a quote. |
| `REOPEN-SECURITY-ERASURE` | Inspect Security/Privacy review of the minimal field/copy inventory, ADR-018 protection, sourced retention and ADR-021-compatible erasure/restore design. Primary data, caches, logs, analytics, backups and provider obligations are all covered; third-party erasure is not assumed from first-party key destruction. |
| `REOPEN-SHARING` | Inspect licensed sharing rights and Privacy/abuse/contract review of any explicitly admitted category/levels, default-deny grants, denial/revocation bounds and erasure tests. Family membership never grants access implicitly. |
| `REOPEN-ADVICE` | Inspect Compliance/advice-boundary review and synthetic negative cases for precisely bounded future display/copy. Score-improvement advice, causal payoff-to-score promises, lending, loan brokering and credit referral remain excluded. |

Reconsideration requires an **explicit new, dated, reviewed and merged superseding
ADR**, its actual decision authority and a consequences-version change that
identifies any newly permitted provider, fields, interfaces and data paths. The
artifact's `NOT_PERFORMED_WHILE_DECLINED` values record that none of this future
diligence or acceptance is claimed here. Changing this release's scope is not an
implicit approval of contracts, spend, collection or sharing.

Only that new merged decision can create separate implementation tickets.
Implementation must start **default-off**; reopening alone enables nothing.
`BUREAU-DEFAULT-OFF-ACCEPTANCE` then requires the artifact's named activation
checks: explicit spending approval, server default-off/entitlement admission,
no fetch on denied/withdrawn consent, permitted-schema-only processing and
fail-closed provider failures, retention/erasure/restore conformance,
default-deny/revocable sharing, no prohibited advice/referral, measured costs
within approved economic limits, and separate Core, QA and affected specialist
review. Runtime evidence belongs to those future tickets before activation,
not to this source decision. These two stages avoid pretending that an
unimplemented feature has passed acceptance.
