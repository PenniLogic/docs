# Participant safety and operating procedures

**Proposal 1.0.0; all operational/qualified approvals pending.**
These are scripts and empty procedures, not legal advice, operational consent
or evidence that a controlled system exists. No procedure is executed by this
change. The narrower #53 rule wins: concepts only; no participant financial
records, recordings, raw notes, transcripts, quotes or device inspection.

## 1. Recruitment and screening

Proposed no-cost route: an independently moderated, general-audience India
community or public research notice board that permits an opt-in notice.
Legal/Privacy must first assess the particular venue and controller notice.
No scraping, bought list, production telemetry, customer/support list, debt
collector/lender referrals, team network, personal contacts or direct messages.
Do not post a notice in a debt-support or coercion-survivor space without a
separate specialist-approved protocol. Venue permission is not participant
consent. No notice or contact is authorized until the README conditions close.

**Draft public notice, not for posting with unfilled fields:**

> We are preparing an optional conversation about two ways a personal finance
> app might organise information. We want critical, positive and uncertain
> reactions equally. This is not a loan, financial advice, a sales call or a
> working product trial. We will not ask to see accounts, messages, balances,
> documents or passwords. Participation is unpaid and voluntary. A session is
> up to 30 minutes, with breaks, text or accessible alternatives where supported.
> If interested, use [approved private opt-in route] for the eligibility and
> privacy notice. Do not post personal information or eligibility answers here.
> The responsible organisation and rights contact are [approved controller
> notice and private participant-initiated contact].

No incentive is budgeted or promised. Any later incentive needs explicit
spending and Legal approval before recruitment, accessible terms unrelated to
favorable answers, and a withdrawal policy that does not punish stopping.
Do not introduce payment details into research records.

**Private screening script, after an accessible screening notice and opt-in:**

> You can decline any question and leave without explaining. Please do not
> describe your finances. Can you privately confirm all of these: you are at
> least 18, currently live in India, currently have consumer debt, and are not
> a team member or someone approached through the team's personal network?
> We only record whether the study's eligibility criteria are met, not which
> criterion failed. No documents or proof are requested.
>
> Is this a safe, private way and time for you to take part? Would another
> supported language, a text format, a break or another accessibility adjustment
> help? You do not need to name a diagnosis or explain your household.

Only an affirmative adult/market/debt/independence attestation is eligible.
Declined or unknown eligibility fails closed without recording its reason.
Screen privately; never request age/date of birth, location below country,
debt type, lender, income, employer, amounts, message contents or credentials.
An inaccessible consent process means **defer and record a coverage gap**, not
pretend that an answer was informed or label that group uninterested.
Do not continue if private participation cannot be confirmed.

For eligible opt-ins, allocate a random 128-bit participant code in the
controlled system, never a name/phone-derived code. The participant receives a
separate confidential withdrawal handle. No actual code, handle, contact or
mapping belongs in Git, issues, Figma, analytics, chat or model prompts.

## 2. Recruitment matrix and limitations

The target for #53 is 16 session starts, not 16 recorded observations. The
matrix is an outreach/coverage plan, **not achieved recruitment**. Use overlapping
dimensions, no identifying cross-tabs and no quota swapping after responses.
Record privately only the non-financial support categories necessary for
delivery; produce privacy-reviewed aggregate coverage before deleting links.
All observed cells currently remain `null` / pending.

| Dimension | Proposed coverage / measure before recruitment | #53 restriction and honest gap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Debt | 16 eligibility attestations covering current consumer debt | No type, balance, repayment or account details; within-debt diversity is unmeasured |
| Income | General-audience routes rather than employed-only venues | No income question or income band retained; income diversity cannot be asserted from this study |
| Permission attitude | Seek varied reactions; aspiration at least 4 open and 4 hesitant/unsure | Measure only after primary preference and the common SMS disclosure, never as an eligibility screener; denial cannot cost a place |
| Financial literacy | Offer plain-language and read-aloud formats; aspiration at least 4 preferring extra explanation and 4 comfortable without it | Format preference is not a literacy diagnosis or a comprehension score |
| Language | Participant-preferred supported language; propose English and Hindi materials with at least 4 sessions each | Translation, independent equivalence review and qualified facilitators are pending; not a product language-rollout decision |
| Disability/access | Accessible text/read-aloud/break options; aspiration at least 4 using an adjustment | No diagnosis required; actual accessible participation and independent accessibility review remain unmet |
| Device | At least 4 constrained/shared-device-safe or text-only sessions and 4 unconstrained sessions | No device identifiers, screenshots or device access; if shared use is unsafe, defer |
| Connectivity | At least 4 low-bandwidth/asynchronous-safe and 4 stable-connection sessions | Do not require video or account creation; asynchronous disclosure/consent must be reviewed first |

These are purposive coverage aspirations, not population weights or statistical
power. Missing targets, unsupported languages/formats, nonresponse and venue
bias must be explicit limitations. Optional responses may be declined without
exclusion. Do not use income/debt proxies to claim the unmeasured cells are met.
If recruitment inadvertently comes from a convenience source, label it, pause
and amend/review the plan; it cannot satisfy #53's independent-participant rule.

For each future study, freeze its target, exclusions, method, matrix, limitations,
retention and the decision it can change **before** recruitment. This #53 plan
cannot be reused as authority for household, medical or credit research.

## 3. Consent, withdrawal and stopping

Before recruitment, fill the controller, researcher, private rights channel,
approved version, language, exact withdrawal deadline and exact deletion
deadlines in a controlled notice. Empty placeholders or a missing responder
block contact. Do not put participant-specific completed forms in this repo.

**Plain-language session consent template:**

> We are comparing two proposed app descriptions, not assessing you or your
> finances. Critical feedback and choosing neither are useful. Participation
> is voluntary; you can skip, pause, close the session or say "stop" at any time
> without giving a reason. This does not affect any service or benefit.
>
> Do not show or tell us balances, debts in detail, accounts, messages,
> credentials or information about anyone else. We will not record audio,
> video, your screen, a transcript, quotes or raw notes. We will retain only
> limited coded reactions to the descriptions and published prices.
>
> Your optional scheduling contact is held separately from those codes,
> encrypted, with access only for the assigned recruiter. Only the assigned
> moderator and analyst can see the coded reactions for this study; designers
> receive anonymous summaries only. No participant information goes into
> public Git or AI/model tools.
>
> Proposed limits, which must be approved and replaced with actual dates before
> you agree: scheduling contacts are deleted within 7 days after the session,
> immediately if you withdraw. Coded reactions and the consent link are kept
> only for withdrawal processing and reviewed synthesis, no longer than 35 days
> after the session, and erased sooner on withdrawal or completed anonymization.
> At that exact expiry they must no longer be readable, including backups and
> exports. There is no recording to retain.
>
> Until [exact approved deadline, 14 days after the fixed field-window end],
> use [private participant-initiated route]
> with your withdrawal handle to remove your linked contribution, without
> explaining why. The route and responsible responder are [completed details].
> We will not notify another household member or send follow-up messages unless
> you separately chose a safe channel and asked for that response.
>
> We will publish only privacy-reviewed anonymous summaries after the withdrawal
> window. Once all links are deleted and a summary is genuinely anonymous, we
> cannot identify and remove your contribution from that published summary.
> We cannot promise to recall copies of a public anonymous report.
>
> Please explain, in your own words, what we would keep and how you could stop.
> You can ask for another explanation or format. Would you like to proceed?

Use a neutral comprehension check, not "you understand, right?". A check failure
means explain again or defer, not coach agreement. Record explicit affirmative
consent to this version, its UTC time and approved expiry in the restricted
consent store. Silence, continued presence, venue permission, an agent's
statement or another household member's agreement is not consent. Re-consent
after material purpose/method/retention changes; prior consent does not migrate.

At session start confirm consent, eligibility, privacy and the stop path
again. Stop immediately for withdrawal, distress, a third party entering,
real financial disclosure, an unintended capture or a policy failure. Do not
investigate household abuse or provide financial/medical advice. Offer a neutral
end/blank screen; no explanation, saved chat, household notification or automatic
follow-up. Escalate process failures to the assigned Privacy/research owner with
only the minimum non-identifying incident category. Keep recruitment paused
until qualified review resolves the failure and the changed source is accepted.

**Withdrawal procedure:** privately verify the participant-held handle without
asking for financial proof; deny analyst access immediately, remove the coded
row and any unpublished linked synthesis, recalculate aggregates, remove contact
mapping and revoke keys/cached grants. Confirm only through a participant-approved
safe channel when requested. If a handle is lost, use the separately approved
rights procedure, not household verification or disclosure that someone enrolled.
No automatic reminders. Reopening an expired or withdrawn grant is forbidden.

## 4. Storage, access, expiry and minimum audit

No storage system is provisioned by this pack. Before contact, the custodian must
demonstrate the following controls with non-participant test data in the actual
approved system. Proposed durations below are upper bounds, not legal approval.

| Data class | Separation / least privilege | Proposed maximum and deletion event |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Scheduling identity mapping | Separate encrypted store and key; assigned recruiter only | Ineligible/declined opt-in: remove immediately after decision; no-show: 7 days after final proposed slot; completed session: 7 days; withdrawal: immediately |
| Eligibility/consent link | Separate encrypted consent store; assigned moderator and rights responder, study-scoped | At most 35 days after session, sooner on withdrawal or completed anonymization; absent/expired/withdrawn consent denies evidence reads |
| Coded evidence | No names, quotes, free text, financial values or actual messages; assigned moderator/analyst only, study-scoped and time-limited | At most 35 days after session, sooner on withdrawal or completed anonymization; expiry also caps every grant |
| Recordings/raw notes | Disabled for #53, including meeting bots, cloud transcripts, captions saved as logs and auto-summaries | Do not create; unintended capture triggers pause and controlled erasure |
| Anonymous aggregate product record | Privacy review, small-cell suppression, no codes, dates/times per person or cross-tabs | Retained as versioned product record only after withdrawal windows and unlinking |
| Minimum operational audit | No code, identity, contact, exact person event or content hash; custodian/auditor only | Proposed 90 days, then delete; approved anonymous product disposition can remain |

The local access predicate tests evidence roles only; actual authentication,
study assignment and trusted-clock enforcement must come from the real store.
Legal, Security, Privacy and designers review policy/anonymous synthesis, not
raw rows by default. Custody/admin privileges do not imply decryption rights.
Access logs must not include reaction content, contact addresses or financial
data. A code is pseudonymous, not anonymous.

Freeze an actual field-window end before contact. The common withdrawal deadline
is 14 days after that end, not an ever-moving last session. The proposed
35-day maximum permits a 14-day field window, 14 days for withdrawal and up to
7 days for privacy-reviewed synthesis of the earliest contribution. Do not
anonymize/publish a contribution before that withdrawal deadline or while a
timely rights request is unresolved. Keep unpublished counts recomputable until
then, apply all withdrawals, review the whole anonymous release and delete
all links immediately on release, even if their maximum expiry is later.
If review cannot finish before expiry, delete on time and report the resulting
evidence gap; never extend retention to save a finding. The controlled record's
`collected_at` is the original session-end time, not a timestamp reset by coding,
copying, re-consent or restoration. These proposed periods require qualified
approval; changing them requires a new reviewed notice before contact.

Enforce expiration on **every read**, not just in a daily cleanup job. At the
deadline revoke decryptability/access across originals, replicas, backups,
exports, local caches and index copies. Physical purge follows the approved
bounded storage procedure; key deletion is evidence only if every recoverable
key/copy is covered. A backup restore must not resurrect expired content.
Prevent uncontrolled downloads. If any copy cannot be made unreadable on time,
do not start the study. A pending deletion job or a local predicate test is not
proof of erasure.

Minimum deletion evidence template (controlled, not participant-specific):
`study protocol version; data class; reporting period; due/succeeded/failed
operation count; storage-control test reference; unresolved exception category`.
No participant code, household link, precise individual timestamp, raw key,
contact or hash of participant content. Privately track withdrawal processing,
oldest evidence age and undispositioned findings as aggregate operational
metrics; never publish an identifying small count. A failed deletion pauses
recruitment and records only the minimal audit; never preserve offending content
merely to demonstrate the failure.

## 5. Synthetic scenarios and reset

The #53 cards need no account, transaction, balance, SMS sample, browser login or
installed app. Present only the versioned text. No real-device permission dialog
is opened and no recording or telemetry is started. This is the safest synthetic
scenario for a framing study: no financial values at all.

Any later prototype study must separately approve its synthetic scenario and
device procedure. Use a research-owned, isolated test profile with no personal
accounts, SIM, contacts, autofill, clipboard history, notifications, backups,
analytics or sync. Label every screen/data set SYNTHETIC TEST MATERIAL; never
derive a fixture from a person's records. Only an authorized custodian may
reset that specific test profile, never a participant's or shared device.
Before and after each session: confirm the exact fixture version, disable
captures, clear the test profile and caches, verify no prior-session content or
notifications reappear offline or after restart, and record a content-free
reset result. If authorized reset or verification is unavailable, stop.
No such device work has been performed here.
