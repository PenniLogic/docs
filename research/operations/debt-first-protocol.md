# Debt-first versus expense-tracking concept protocol

**T-RES-01 / #53; proposal 1.0.0; UNRUN; method review pending.**
This is a pre-study protocol, not the finding required by #53. Recruitment and
sessions remain prohibited until the [start conditions](README.md) are accepted.
All thresholds are proposed **before** data, awaiting qualified independent
review; none has been agreed by a fabricated reviewer.

## 1. Question, sample and freeze

Decision: retain, revise or stop the unqualified debt-first positioning before
the expensive debt experience is built. Also challenge the paid-plan and
SMS-capture assumptions. Do not change confirmed prices, release a product,
claim market size or infer longitudinal retention from a concept preference.
This is prior evidence for R2, not a replacement for the later real retention
kill criterion.

Target: 16 independently recruited, eligible, consenting India adults with
current consumer debt; maximum 16 session starts over a proposed 14-day field
window. No start date exists. A session uses no personal financial data.
Eligibility, exclusions, coverage aspirations and limitations are in the
[procedures](procedures.md) and [plan](concept-study.plan.json).

Freeze the protocol/cards, consent language, source price revision, order
schedule, definitions, thresholds, time window and reviewed translations before
recruitment. Record the accepted commit and reviewer decisions. A change requires
a dated amendment and review before affected contact; do not rewrite earlier
criteria or pool changed versions silently. The fixed field-window end also
sets the common withdrawal deadline 14 days later; synthesis gets at most 7
further days within the earliest evidence's 35-day retention cap. Do not ask the
permission-attitude question before the primary comparison to fill matrix cells.

Order slots are **allocations, not participants**. Use the fixed counterbalanced
block `DE, ED, ED, DE` four times (D = debt card, E = expense card). Allocate
consecutive slots at consented session start, never by attitude or observed
preference. No-show before start leaves the next slot available. After a card is
shown the slot is consumed even if the participant stops; do not replace that
slot, reorder or extend the window in response to results. Report attrition,
order imbalance and schedule deviations. This is counterbalancing, **not random
sampling or a claim of randomized allocation**.

Use the same facilitator wording, medium, available reading time, illustration
level (text only), typography and supporting feature/price/permission facts for
both cards. "First card" and "second card" follow the allocated order, not a
permanent favorable label. Do not reveal a preferred hypothesis or the product's
debt-first tagline before primary preference. Translate both together, back-check
meaning independently and do not pool an unreviewed translation.

## 2. Before-study falsification and decision rules

For analysis, N counts eligible, unwithdrawn sessions that saw both cards and
were offered the primary preference question under the frozen protocol.
"Equal", "neither", "unsure" and "skip" remain explicit responses in that
denominator; they are not allocated to debt or discarded. Predefined exclusions
are ineligibility, withdrawal, incomplete two-card exposure or a material
protocol/safety failure. Record aggregate exclusions without financial details.
Missing price/SMS answers remain skipped, never favorable. Do not select a
responsive subgroup retrospectively.

Apply these rules in order:

| Rule fixed before study | Interpretation / action |
| --- | --- |
| Any consent, privacy, coercion, capture or access failure | Stop the affected session, pause recruitment, protect/erase data under the procedures and get qualified review; no favorable conclusion overrides safety |
| N below 12, or fewer than 5 analyzable sessions in either order | Inconclusive / order not assessable; do not claim validated positioning |
| Opposite debt-versus-expense leaders in the two orders, or debt-preference rates differ by at least 25 percentage points | Order-sensitive: inconclusive, investigate and pre-register a new test; never pool away the contradiction |
| Without the above problems, debt does not beat expense, or debt is chosen by no more than half of N | The proposed strong debt-first wedge is not supported; stop treating it as established and disposition a positioning/specification change |
| Debt reaches at least 62.5% of N AND exceeds half of each order cohort | Candidate support for further testing only; not product/retention acceptance |
| Any remaining preference result | Mixed/inconclusive; report it as such and require a reasoned retest/change decision |
| Fewer than half of N choose any published paid option after the full terms/disclosure | Paid-conversion premise is challenged; report hypothetical intent and raise a pricing/packaging review or explicitly decline it with reasons; prices do not change here |
| Fewer than half of N say they would allow the described SMS permission | Capture/permission premise is challenged; preserve permission-denied manual use and reconsider capture dependence, never pressure consent |

Compare rates using their actual denominators. The offline order check uses
integer counts and includes ties/skips; it returns "candidate_support",
"not_supported", "order_sensitive" or "inconclusive", not an approval.
No significance, prevalence, power or population estimate is implied by these
decision thresholds. Even candidate support cannot erase a paid/SMS contradiction,
an unmet matrix target or an accessibility problem.

After fieldwork and withdrawal expiry, the qualified research lead reviews
primary versus post-disclosure preference, both order cohorts and all exclusions.
Product and the accepted #64 decision authority must record a ticketed change,
an explicit reasoned decline, a pre-registered retest or a stop decision.
An undispositioned contradiction blocks a "validated" claim and silent continuation.
This study cannot by itself authorize frontend delivery or waive any design gate.

## 3. Neutral discussion guide (up to 30 minutes)

**Opening, consent and stop check (5 minutes).**
Read the approved notice and confirm eligibility, privacy, accessible comprehension
and affirmative consent. Say:

> We are comparing two descriptions. Neither is the correct answer. Critical,
> mixed or uncertain reactions and choosing neither are useful. Please discuss
> the descriptions, not your own accounts, income, messages or balances. We
> will not record this conversation. You can skip or stop.

**Common facts, once before either card.**

> Both concepts would offer manual entry, spending categories and budgets, a
> debt list and illustrative payoff scenarios. The difference here is what the
> first view emphasises. Calculations would be scenarios, not financial advice
> or a promise. These are descriptions, not evidence that the app is built.

**Debt card, verbatim when allocated.**

> A personal finance app that starts with money you owe. The first view puts your
> debt list and payoff scenarios first. Spending categories and budgets are
> available alongside them. You decide what to record and which view to use.
> This is a concept, not a working service.

**Expense card, verbatim when allocated.**

> A personal finance app that starts with everyday spending. The first view puts
> spending categories and budgets first. Your debt list and payoff scenarios are
> available alongside them. You decide what to record and which view to use.
> This is a concept, not a working service.

**After each card, identical probes (about 3 minutes each).**

> In your own words, what would this app put first? What is clear or unclear?
> What, if anything, would be useful or not useful about that starting point?
> What would you want to know before deciding whether to use it?

**Primary comparison, before price/SMS detail (3 minutes).**

> Which starting point, if either, would you choose: the first, the second, both
> equally, neither, or are you unsure? You may skip. What about the descriptions
> led you there? What might make that choice unsuitable?

Record the code for the framing, not "first = debt". Do not ask for a debt story,
a current spending example, balances, saving potential, a lender or ability to
afford a bill. Do not say "wouldn't a payoff date motivate you?" or praise one
answer. If uncodable detail is volunteered, retain only `not_coded`, no quotation.

## 4. Published India prices, not an invented willingness-to-pay price

Show this same information for both concepts. Source:
[product specification section 4.1 and BYOK guards 4.3](../../product/01-product-spec.md#41-confirmed-pricing-from-unit-economics-analysis-2026-09)
at base `3e4afcb9575badf8669a50136da69fcc6f634500`.
These are the published September 2026 prices, not an actual checkout offer.
Recheck the accepted source before freezing; a change requires renewed review.

| Tier | Monthly price (INR) | Published annual equivalent (INR per month) | Published AI allowance / caveat |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Free | 0 | No annual price stated | 5 Q&A/month |
| Pro | 299 | 199; source explicitly states 2,388/year | 150 Q&A + 30 summaries |
| Pro+ | 499 | 349 | 500 Q&A |
| BYOK Pro | 149 | 99 | Own provider key and its separate charges; see qualification below |

Do not call the annual equivalent a month-to-month payment. Do not invent
Pro+/BYOK annual totals, tax, refund or cancellation terms absent from this
source. BYOK's table says "Unlimited (their key)", but the same specification
4.3 states an approximately 500 Q&A/month orchestration soft cap and excludes
priority support, family sharing and premium integrations. Disclose that
qualification rather than promise unlimited use. Unclear billing/feature terms
are a recorded limitation, not something the moderator supplies from imagination.
Core manual tracking and debt payoff basics remain free; do not imply a paid
subscription is necessary to see the debt concept.

**Neutral price probe (5 minutes).**

> Given these published options and the information that is still missing,
> would you choose Free, a named monthly or annual paid option, none, or are you
> unsure? You can skip. What is clear or unclear about the choice? What further
> information would you need? Please do not tell us your income, budget or bills.

Record a plan-choice code, not a rupee amount, payment instrument, maximum budget
or a promise to purchase. This is hypothetical intent, not revealed demand.
Do not upsell, anchor with supposed savings, discount prices or collect payment.

## 5. Plain SMS disclosure and reactions (5 minutes)

> On Android, allowing the SMS permission would let the app read text messages
> on the phone to look for financial transactions. Access is broad; financial
> filtering happens after access. The proposed design keeps raw messages on the
> device and may sync structured transaction information to your account's
> server. This description is not proof that the controls or Play approval exist.
>
> If you deny SMS access, SMS automatic capture would not work, but manual entry
> and core tracking would remain usable. Other optional permissions would be
> separate choices. Both concepts have these same rules. We are not requesting
> any permission or asking to see a phone today.
>
> In your own words, what would allowing or denying this mean? Would you allow,
> deny, or be unsure about this request? You can skip. What, if anything, concerns
> you or remains unclear? Please do not show us a message.

Record allow/deny/unsure/skip, including refusal without persuasion. Do not
reframe denial as a misunderstanding to correct into acceptance. Clarify only
factual questions with the same script; unsupported assertions are "not known".
This is a stated reaction to a permission, not an observed grant on a device.

**Closing (remaining time; shorten any earlier section if requested).**

> After hearing the same prices and permission details, has your choice of
> starting point changed, stayed the same, or become uncertain? What have we
> missed? Would you like any of your contribution removed? Here is the private
> withdrawal route and its deadline.

No follow-up or household contact without a new, safe, participant-controlled
opt-in. Immediately encode only allowlisted reactions; do not transcribe or
feed the conversation to a model. The final report gives contradictions,
uncertainty and support equally visible sections.
